================================================================================
NAVIGATION TIPS
================================================================================

1. The main route to Mordor typically goes:
   Shire → Bree → Rivendell → Moria → Lothlórien → Rohan → Gondor → Mordor

2. Alternative routes:
   - Through Fangorn Forest (from Rohan)
   - Through Mirkwood (northern route)
   - Direct to Minas Morgul (from Gondor)

3. Key landmarks on the way:
   - Bag End (start)
   - Bree (first major town)
   - Rivendell (rest and supplies)
   - Moria (dangerous underground passage)
   - Lothlórien (elven sanctuary)
   - Rohan (plains and horses)
   - Minas Tirith (Gondor capital)
   - Black Gate or Cirith Ungol (entrances to Mordor)

4. Remember: All locations are reachable from Bag End!
