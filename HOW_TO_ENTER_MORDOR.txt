                    HOW TO ENTER MORDOR
                    From The Black Gate to Plains of Mordor
================================================================================

You've reached The Black Gate of Mordor! To enter Mordor, follow this path:

================================================================================

         To: Durin's Chamber

         To: Rhosgobel - Home of Radagast

Step 3:  Type: southeast
         To: Hidden Path in Fangorn

Step 4:  Type: southeast
         To: Annúminas Tower

Step 5:  Type: southeast
         To: Buckland Cellar

Step 6:  Type: southeast
         To: Mayor's Office

Step 7:  Type: west
         To: Buckland Kitchen

Step 8:  Type: southwest
         To: Annúminas Ruins

Step 9:  Type: southwest
         To: Confluence of Rivers

Step 10: Type: southwest
         To: Mirkwood Depths - Spider Warrens

Step 11: Type: southwest
         To: Third Level - Minas Tirith

Step 12: Type: southwest
         To: Plains of Mordor [mordor_plains]
         Coordinates: (-7, -5, 0)

✅ YOU HAVE ENTERED MORDOR!

================================================================================
                    NOTE: DIRECT PATH AVAILABLE
================================================================================

There are 11 rooms along the same vertical line (x=-7) between the Black Gate
and Plains of Mordor that could form a direct south path:

1. The Black Gate (-7, 6)
2. Tunnel Exit (-7, 5)
3. Cirith Ungol (-7, 4)
4. Morgul Vale (-7, 3)
5. Rath Dínen (-7, 2)
6. Gates of Minas Tirith (-7, 1)
7. Pelennor Fields (-7, 0)
8. White Tower (-7, -1)
9. House of the Stewards (-7, -2)
10. Morgul Pass (-7, -3)
11. Shelob's Lair (-7, -4)
12. Plains of Mordor (-7, -5)

If these were connected with "south" exits, you could simply type "south" 
11 times from the Black Gate to reach the Plains of Mordor directly.

Currently, these rooms exist but aren't connected in a straight line, so you
must use the detour path shown above.

================================================================================
                    OTHER MORDOR LOCATIONS
================================================================================

Once in Plains of Mordor, you can reach other locations:

- Approach to Barad-dûr: Continue southwest from Plains of Mordor
- Mount Doom: Various paths lead north/northeast from interior Mordor
- Barad-dûr: Located in the heart of Mordor
