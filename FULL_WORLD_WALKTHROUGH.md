# Full World Walkthrough Guide

This guide will take you through every major region in Middle Earth Adventure Game.

## Starting Point: The Shire

1. **Start the game** - You begin in Bag End
2. **Collect items**: `take walking stick`, `take lembas bread`
3. **Go south** to Hobbiton Square
4. **Collect**: `take silver coin`
5. **Go east** to The Green Dragon Inn
6. **Collect**: `take health potion`, `take old map`
7. **Go west** back to Hobbiton Square
8. **Go south** to Brandywine Bridge

## Journey to Rivendell

9. **Go east** to Bree Gate
10. **Go east** to Bree Square
11. **Collect**: `take brass key` (if available)
12. **Go east** to East Road from Bree
13. **Go east** to The Trollshaws
14. **Go east** to Ford of Bruinen
15. **Go east** to Gates of Rivendell
16. **Go east** to Hall of Fire - Rivendell
17. **Explore Rivendell**:
    - `go north` to Library of Rivendell
    - `go south` to Rivendell Forge
    - Collect items: `take mithril mail`, `take elvish blade`

## Entering Moria

18. **Go south** from Rivendell Forge to Hollin Gate
19. **Collect**: `take mithril fragment`
20. **Go south** to Doors of Durin
21. **Solve puzzle**: `solve gateway_of_moria mellon` (or the correct answer)
22. **Go east** to First Hall of Moria (Moria Entrance)
23. **Collect**: `take old torch`, `take dwarven helm`
24. **Go down** to Mines Level 1
25. **Explore Moria levels**:
    - `go down` multiple times to explore different levels
    - `go east` to Twenty-First Hall
    - `go north` to Seventh Level
    - `go down` through levels 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1
    - `go east` to Bridge of Khazad-dûm
    - `go east` to East Gate Approach

## Lothlórien

26. **Continue east** from Moria exit
27. **Go north** to Lothlórien Border
28. **Go south** to Cerin Amroth
29. **Go north** to Caras Galadhon - City of the Trees
30. **Go up** to Galadriel's Court
31. **Collect**: `take phial of galadriel`, `take elven rope`
32. **Go west** to Mirror Chamber
33. **Go down** back to Caras Galadhon

## Fangorn Forest

34. **Go south** from Lothlórien area
35. **Go east** to Fangorn Eaves
36. **Go east** to Wellinghall (Treebeard's home)
37. **Explore**: Look for Ents and Treebeard

## Rohan

38. **Go south** from Fangorn
39. **Go west** to Rohan Plains
40. **Go east** to Road to Edoras
41. **Go up** to Gates of Edoras
42. **Go north** to Meduseld (Golden Hall)
43. **Collect**: `take rohirric sword`, `take horn of rohan`
44. **Go west** to Harrowdale
45. **Go south** to Helm's Deep
46. **Explore Helm's Deep**: Look around, collect items
47. **Go east** from Rohan to Isengard
48. **Go up** to Orthanc Tower

## Paths of the Dead

49. **Go south** from Rohan
50. **Go south** to Dunharrow
50. **Go east** to Paths of the Dead entrance
51. **Go east** to Dead City
52. **Explore**: Encounter Oathbreakers

## Gondor - Minas Tirith

53. **Go south** from Paths of the Dead
54. **Go east** to Pelennor Fields
55. **Go west** to Gates of Minas Tirith
56. **Go up** to First Level - Minas Tirith
57. **Climb through all 7 levels**:
    - `go up` to Second Level
    - `go up` to Third Level (Houses of Healing)
    - `go up` to Fourth Level
    - `go up` to Fifth Level (Great Hall)
    - `go up` to Sixth Level (Citadel)
    - `go up` to White Tower
58. **Collect**: `take palantir of minas tirith`
59. **Go down** to exit tower
60. **Go east** to Osgiliath Ruins

## Mordor

61. **Go east** from Osgiliath to Morgul Vale
62. **Go east** to Cirith Ungol
63. **Go down** to Shelob's Lair
64. **Fight Shelob** (if present): `attack shelob`
65. **Go east** to exit tunnel
66. **Go east** to Plains of Mordor
67. **Go east** to The Black Gate of Mordor
68. **Fight guards** (if needed): `attack orc warrior`
69. **Go east** to Barad-dûr Approach
70. **Go east** to Barad-dûr Base
71. **Go up** to Barad-dûr Summit
72. **Fight Sauron** (final boss): `attack sauron`
73. **Go west** back to Plains of Mordor
74. **Go south** to Mount Doom Approach
75. **Go up** to Mount Doom Summit
76. **Solve final puzzle**: `solve destroy_ring yes` (or correct answer)

## Commands to Use Throughout

- `look` - See your surroundings
- `inventory` or `i` - Check your items
- `stats` - See your level, HP, XP, etc.
- `achievements` - View unlocked achievements
- `recipes` - See crafting recipes
- `save` - Save your progress
- `attack <enemy>` - Fight enemies
- `take <item>` - Pick up items
- `use <item>` - Use/equip items
- `craft <item1> <item2>` - Combine items

## Tips

- **Level up** by defeating enemies and solving puzzles
- **Collect items** - many are needed for puzzles and crafting
- **Save frequently** - use `save` command
- **Check achievements** - see your progress
- **Explore thoroughly** - many secrets and items to find
- **Fight strategically** - use potions when low on HP
- **Solve puzzles** - they give XP and unlock areas

## Expected Journey Stats

By the end of this walkthrough, you should have:
- Visited 50+ different rooms
- Collected 20+ items
- Reached level 5-10 (depending on combat)
- Unlocked multiple achievements
- Defeated several bosses
- Solved major puzzles

Enjoy your journey through Middle Earth! 🗺️
